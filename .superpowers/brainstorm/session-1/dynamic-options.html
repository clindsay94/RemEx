Defining "Dynamic" Experience

How should the themes evolve during use?

A

Reactive State-Based

The UI colors and effects shift based on hardware metrics (e.g., the glow turns red when CPU > 90%, or glass blurs more when RAM is full).

B

Atmospheric Motion

Ambient animated gradients and "breathing" glass effects that happen constantly, creating a sense of life regardless of the system state.

C

Hybrid Approach

Constant subtle ambient motion that intensifies or shifts color during high-load/warning states.
